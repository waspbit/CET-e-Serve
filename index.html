CET e-SERVE

Electronic Service: Request, Suggestion & Feedback Form

CET e-SERVE provides students of the College of Engineering and Technology with a formal channel for submitting service requests, suggestions, feedback, and concerns to the CET Student Council. No account or login is required, and the disclosure of personal information is entirely voluntary.

Proceed to the Form

How it works

  1. Complete the formSelect the type and category, then describe your request, suggestion, or concern.
  2. Choose how to submitSubmit anonymously, or provide your details. All contact information is optional.
  3. Receive a reference numberKeep it for your records. Authorized CET personnel will review your submission.

Your privacy

How your submission is handled.

Anonymous Submission

No name or contact information is requested or recorded when you submit anonymously.

Voluntary Disclosure

Provide as much or as little personal information as you wish. All contact fields are optional.

Authorized Review

Submissions are accessible only to designated CET Student Council officers and administrators.

Data Privacy Notice. Anonymity under this system means that no identifying or contact information is requested or stored by the application. The website hosting platform may retain standard technical logs that are beyond the control of this form; accordingly, absolute anonymity cannot be guaranteed. All submissions are handled in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and applicable university policies.
