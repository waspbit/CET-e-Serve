CET e-SERVE

Electronic Service: Request, Suggestion & Feedback Form

CET e-SERVE provides students of the College of Engineering and Technology with a formal channel for submitting service requests, suggestions, feedback, and concerns to the CET Student Council. No account or login is required, and the disclosure of personal information is entirely voluntary.

Proceed to the Form

Types of Submissions

Service Request
Formal requests for assistance or services from the College.
Suggestion
Proposals to improve programs, facilities, or activities.
Feedback
Comments on existing services and student experiences.
Concern or Complaint
Matters that require attention or resolution.

Anonymous Submission

Students may submit anonymously. In such cases, no name or contact information is requested or recorded by this system.

Voluntary Disclosure

Students who choose to identify themselves may provide as much or as little information as they wish. All contact fields are optional.

Authorized Review

Submissions are accessible only to designated CET Student Council officers and authorized administrators.

Data Privacy Notice

Anonymity under this system means that no identifying or contact information is requested or stored by the application. The website hosting platform may retain standard technical logs that are beyond the control of this form; accordingly, absolute anonymity cannot be guaranteed. All submissions are handled in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and applicable university policies.
